# CJFR constraints (checked 2026-10-09)

Sources: CSP general author guidelines + CJFR journal-specific section
(`https://cdnsciencepub.com/journal/cjfr/authors`, saved copy `<workspace>/tmp/cjfr-author-guidelines.txt`);
CJFR French guidelines PDF "Last updated November 2025"; CSP submission-process and
data-availability pages.

1. **Article type: Research article** — typically ≤ 6000 words Introduction → end of Discussion,
   ≤ 50 references (Note: 3500 words / 30 refs / 150-word abstract; Methods Paper: 5000 words
   *including* refs, tables, figures).
2. **Abstract** ≤ 200 words, one paragraph, no citations/tables/figures; rationale, objectives,
   methods, findings, impact; English and/or French. Keywords listed after the abstract.
3. **No hard figure/table cap**, but every figure/table must be necessary; extra material goes to
   supplementary files (Fig. S1, Table S1…, cited by footnote; named `<MS-ID>suppla`, …).
4. **Format:** double-spaced, single column, page numbers, continuous line numbers, Letter or A4.
5. **Order:** title page → abstract → keywords → Introduction, Materials and methods, Results,
   Discussion → acknowledgements → references; figures/tables/captions inline or after the text
   or as separate files.
6. **Title page:** informative title with keywords; full names, affiliations (dept, institution,
   city, province, country); corresponding author + email; competing interests and funding
   statements also on the title page.
7. **References: Harvard** (author year), alphabetical, unnumbered, DOIs/links where possible;
   CSP style (e.g. "Philip, M.S. 1994. Title. Journal, vol: pages."). Submitted/unpublished work
   is not listed (cite in text); datasets cited in the reference list as
   "Author. Year. Title (version) [dataset]. Repository. PID. Link."
8. **Data availability statement** (required, before references): repository, PID (DOI), link,
   reuse conditions. CJFR partners with **Dryad** (free deposit of data *and code* through end of
   2026 via provisional URL sent on submission). No separate code-availability rule; we state code
   availability in the same statement.
9. **Author contribution statement** (required) with CRediT roles, authors by initials; roles also
   entered in ScholarOne.
10. **Competing interests** (required; "The authors declare there are no competing interests.")
    and **Funding** statement (agency in full + grant numbers, or "no specific funding").
11. **Generative AI:** disclose all AI-assisted tools (name + model/version) at submission;
    writing assistance in Acknowledgements; use in research/code in Methods.
12. **Equations** numbered in parentheses flush **left**; every symbol defined; SI units.
13. **Tables** must be editable (Word/Excel at production; LaTeX acceptable for review);
    footnotes *, †, ‡ … or a,b,c. **Figures** ≥ 300 dpi at production (PDF/EPS/TIFF preferred);
    captions self-contained.
14. **Source format:** Word, PDF or LaTeX accepted at submission (no mandatory template); LaTeX
    images referenced without `./` paths, all files in one folder, `.bbl` may need inlining for
    ScholarOne conversion. Editable source (DOCX/LaTeX) required after acceptance.
15. **Submission system: ScholarOne Manuscripts** (CJFR site); suggested referees optional
    (no collaborators); optional graphical abstract (≤ 40 × 85 mm); statistical reporting: give
    n/N for proportions and state assumptions (our deterministic design reports descriptives).
